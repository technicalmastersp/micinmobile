1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Mic in Mobile (the “app”) handles information. The app is published by TechnicalMasterSP (“we”, “us”, “our”). On Google Play it is listed as “Live Mic for Bluetooth Speaker” (package name com.technicalmastersp.mic).

The app turns your phone into a live microphone and plays your voice through the phone speaker, a wired headset, or a Bluetooth audio device that is already connected to your phone. By using the app you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the app.

2. Your microphone and your voice

Your voice is the most sensitive thing the app touches, so here is exactly what happens to it:

  • The microphone is used only while you have turned the microphone ON inside the app.
  • Audio is processed live, in memory, on your device — gain, bass and treble, a feedback guard, and (when enabled) Android’s built-in echo cancellation, noise suppression and automatic gain control — and is then sent straight to the output you selected.
  • The app does not record your voice to a file, does not upload it to any server, does not transcribe or analyse it, and does not use it for advertising.
  • Over Bluetooth, Android sends the audio to your paired device. That connection is between your phone and your device; it does not pass through us.
  • While the microphone is active the app shows a notification, and Android may show its own microphone indicator.

Because a microphone can pick up other people nearby, please use the app responsibly and in line with the Terms and Conditions.

3. Information the app does not collect

The app itself does not ask for, collect, or transmit to us:

  • your name, e-mail address, phone number, or any account or password — there is no sign-up and no account;
  • your contacts, photos, files, camera, SMS, or call history;
  • your GPS or precise location;
  • recordings or transcripts of your voice.

We do not run our own servers for the app, and we do not use our own analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. The one exception is advertising, described in Section 5.

4. Information stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data in private app storage on your phone. We never receive it.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is supported by ads. It uses Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show banner ads and full-screen (interstitial) ads.

When an ad is requested, Google may collect and use information such as:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, and basic device and app information;
  • which ads were shown and how you interacted with them;
  • performance and diagnostic data, including crash logs.

Google uses this information to deliver, measure and improve ads, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Google acts as an independent controller of this data under its own policies; we receive aggregated ad-performance reports, not the personal data Google collects. Microphone audio is never part of any ad request.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and How Google uses AdMob data.

Your ad choices. In your device’s Settings (usually under Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads, depending on your device and Android version) you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization. Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them less tailored to you.

6. Permissions the app uses

The app asks only for what its features need. You can review or revoke permissions at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Mic in Mobile → Permissions.

PermissionWhy it is needed

Internet and network state

8. If you contact support

If you e-mail technicalmastersp@gmail.com, we receive your e-mail address and whatever you include in your message or attachments. We use it only to answer you and to improve the app, and we keep it only as long as needed to handle your request and for a reasonable period afterwards. Your e-mail provider processes the message under its own privacy policy. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

9. Data retention and deletion

We hold no user data on servers, so there is nothing for us to delete there. Data on your phone is removed when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. Data held by Google for advertising is kept according to Google’s own policies (see Section 5). To delete e-mails you have sent us, write to us at the address in Section 15.

10. Security

Your voice is processed on your device and is not sent to us. Settings are kept in private app storage. The app is configured to block unencrypted (HTTP) network traffic. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we limit what the app collects precisely so there is little to protect.

  • Clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Manage ad personalization as described in Section 5.
  • Contact us about any privacy question or request.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. Because the app has no accounts and we hold no profile about you, these requests mostly concern e-mails you have sent us; for data Google collects for ads, please use Google’s own controls.
