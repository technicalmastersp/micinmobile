3. What the App does and what it needs

The App captures sound from your phone’s microphone and plays it live through the phone speaker, a wired headset, or a Bluetooth audio device already connected through Android.

To work, the App needs Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer, a working microphone, and the permissions described in the Privacy Policy. The microphone works offline; an internet connection is used only to load ads.

Audio delay (latency) and sound quality depend on your phone, your speaker or headset, and the Bluetooth connection. Latency is never zero, and we do not promise that the App works with every phone or audio device.
